# Law Firm Intake Form Field Guide

<p class="by">Field-by-field advice for the contact and intake forms on a law firm website: what to ask, what to leave for the call, and why. By <a href="https://legalwebagency.com/">Legal Web Agency</a>.</p>

The intake form is where most website leads are won or lost. A form that asks for too much loses people; one that asks for too little sends the intake team calls it cannot qualify. This guide goes through each common field and says whether it belongs on the website form. For a ready-made version, start from our [law firm intake form template](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-intake-form-template/), and for the reasoning behind it see [what a law firm intake form should ask](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-intake-form/).

## Fields that belong on every form

| Field | Why | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Needed to call back | One field, not first/last |
| Phone | Fastest way to reach a lead | Mark it as preferred contact |
| Email | Backup contact and confirmation | Optional if phone is given |
| Practice area or "What do you need help with?" | Routes the lead | A short dropdown, plus "Other" |
| Short description | Lets intake prepare | 2-3 lines, no minimum length |

## Fields to leave for the phone call

- Date of birth, address and Social Security number. Never ask for these on a public form.
- Opposing party names. Collect them on the call for the conflict check.
- Detailed medical or financial information.
- Income questions, except where a practice must screen early (see below).

## Practice-specific screening questions

One extra question can save the intake team hours, if it is the right one:

- **Personal injury:** "When did the accident happen?" helps spot deadline issues. See [personal injury leads](https://legalwebagency.com/personal-injury-leads/).
- **Bankruptcy:** a simple debt range helps sort Chapter 7 and 13 inquiries. See [bankruptcy attorney leads](https://legalwebagency.com/bankruptcy-attorney-leads/).
- **Mass torts:** product or exposure type. See [mass tort attorney leads](https://legalwebagency.com/mass-tort-attorney-leads/).
- **Any practice:** a location question keeps out-of-state inquiries from taking slots. See [filtering unqualified legal leads](https://legalwebagency.com/filter-unqualified-legal-leads/).

## Required notices next to the form

- "Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship."
- "Please do not send confidential information through this form."
- A link to the privacy policy.

## After the form is sent

1. Show a clear confirmation and what happens next.
2. Send an automatic email or text within minutes.
3. Call back fast. Leads cool quickly; [legal intake services](https://legalwebagency.com/legal-intake-services/) can cover evenings and weekends.
4. Offer a booking link for people who prefer to pick a time. See [online consultation booking](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-online-consultation-booking/).
5. Track every submission as a conversion. See [conversion tracking for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-conversion-tracking/).

## Where the form lives

The form belongs on the contact page and at the end of every practice page, next to a tappable phone number.
